FAQ

Common questions

How long does delivery take? ▾
Once your order is placed, your book is printed within 24 hours and shipped worldwide. UK and US orders typically arrive in 3–5 business days. International orders may take 5–10 days.
Is the story really unique? ▾
Yes — completely. Our AI generates a brand new story and illustrations for every single order. Even if you order two books with the same name and theme, they'll be entirely different stories.
Can I preview before I pay? ▾
Absolutely. We generate a free preview of your book — including sample pages and illustrations — before you pay a single penny. You only pay when you're happy with what you see.
What age is it suitable for? ▾
We create books for children aged 3–8. Our AI adjusts the reading level, vocabulary, and story complexity based on the age you provide.
What if I'm not happy with my order? ▾
We offer a 100% satisfaction guarantee. If there's a print quality issue or the book arrives damaged, we'll reprint and reship at no extra cost.
Can I order in bulk for a class or event? ▾
Yes! We offer discounted bulk pricing for schools, birthday parties, and events. Contact us for orders of 10 or more books.
